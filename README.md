# Explainable AI (XAI)

This repository is a work in progress containing notes and code on Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI), developed as part of the research activities within the Doctoral Program in Electrical and Computer Engineering (PDEEC) at the Faculty of Engineering of the University of Porto (FEUP).

The materials are intended for educational purposes and do not constitute an official publication of FEUP. Feedback, corrections, and suggestions for improvement are welcome.
